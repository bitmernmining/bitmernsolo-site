Quick setup

Your unit ships with stock, unmodified AxeOS / ESP-Miner, pre-configured for Bitmern Solo (flat 1% pool fee — you keep 99%). Fully unlockable. Not an official Bitaxe.

  1. Power the board and join its Wi-Fi setup AP (see AxeOS / device label).
  2. Open the AxeOS UI and confirm Wi-Fi for your home network.
  3. Stratum should already point at Bitmern Solo, e.g. stratum+tcp://btc.bitmernsolo.com:3102 (other coins use their public *.bitmernsolo.com hosts).
  4. Username: YOUR_WALLET_ADDRESS.workerName · Password: x (LTC merge: doge=YourDogeAddress).
  5. Save, reboot if prompted, and watch shares on the Bitmern Solo dashboard.
Pool fee is 1% on block rewards. You keep 99%. Firmware is unlockable — change pool, Wi-Fi, or flash anytime. Credits to the open AxeOS / ESP-Miner projects.
